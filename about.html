The person behind the practice

Meet Dibia.

Aderonke Oluseye-Adegbami is a holistic health practitioner, author and speaker. Her work brings whole-person wellbeing into the everyday.

Her point of view

Wellbeing belongs in everyday life.

Known as Dibia, Aderonke connects whole-person wellbeing with the choices we make every day — from how we eat to the habits that shape how we care for ourselves.

As a practitioner, author and speaker, she shares a grounded perspective on nutrition, daily habits and the many ways we can show up for our wellbeing.
