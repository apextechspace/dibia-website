Meet Dibia

Wellbeing in everyday life

Aderonke Oluseye-Adegbami, known as Dibia, is a holistic health practitioner, author and speaker. Her work connects whole-person wellbeing with the choices we make every day, from how we eat to the habits that shape how we care for ourselves.
